# Enterprise Reality Programme

# Strategic Backlog

## Purpose

This document records architectural initiatives that have been consciously deferred.

Items contained within this backlog have been reviewed and approved in principle but are **not part of the current critical delivery path**.

The backlog exists to preserve architectural ideas without allowing them to introduce scope creep.

---

# Guiding Principle

> Good architecture is not only deciding what to build.
>
> It is also deciding what not to build yet.

The Enterprise Reality Programme shall prioritise work that directly advances its constitutional roadmap.

Strategic enhancements shall be deferred until they provide clear value without delaying the programme's principal objectives.

# EPIC-WEB-5

# Enterprise Reality Portal

**Status**

Approved

**Priority**

Strategic Enhancement

**Implementation**

Deferred

---

## Vision

Transform the Enterprise Reality Website into the permanent constitutional portal of the Enterprise Reality Programme.

The Portal shall become the primary mechanism through which readers discover, navigate and understand Enterprise Reality.

---

## Business Justification

The existing website already fulfils its primary purpose:

- publishing constitutional works;
- publishing architecture;
- supporting discoverability.

The Portal represents an enhancement rather than a prerequisite for Release v0.6.0.

Implementation is intentionally deferred to preserve focus on the Enterprise Automation Engine.

# Architectural Decision

## ADR-WEB-005 (Proposed)

### Title

Defer Enterprise Reality Portal implementation until completion of the Enterprise Automation Engine.

### Decision

The current website provides sufficient capability to publish and maintain the Enterprise Reality Canon.

Portal evolution is approved as a strategic enhancement but removed from the current delivery path.

### Rationale

The Enterprise Automation Engine represents the next major capability of the Enterprise Reality Programme.

Completing that capability provides greater strategic value than enhancing the website at this stage.

The Portal architecture remains preserved within this backlog for future implementation.

---

# Exit Criteria

Implementation of EPIC-WEB-5 shall commence only when:

- Release v0.6.0 has reached a stable state;
- the Enterprise Reality Canon has expanded sufficiently to benefit from enhanced navigation;
- the Portal provides demonstrable value beyond the existing publication website.
