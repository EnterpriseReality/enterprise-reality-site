# ER-WEB-006 — Website v2 Information Architecture Amendment

**Publication ID:** ER-WEB-006  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved Authority Amendment  
**Classification:** Public  
**Programme:** Enterprise Reality  
**Owner:** Enterprise Reality Programme  
**Effective from:** Website v2 implementation authorisation

---

## 1. Purpose

This amendment resolves the Website v1 information-architecture conflict. It
adopts the accepted Website v2 semantic architecture without reopening the
accepted product narrative or changing the non-conflicting authority of
ER-CON-002, ER-WEB-001, ER-WEB-002, ER-WEB-003 or ER-WEB-004.

## 2. Narrow supersession

This amendment supersedes only the Website v1 requirements that prescribe or
require the previous top-level navigation, route grouping, Industries
catalogue, standalone Roadmap, or separate Research and Architecture public
areas. This includes ER-WEB-001 scope and deliverable statements, and
ER-WEB-002 section 6, only to that extent.

All other requirements in those publications remain in force, including
truth-before-marketing, public evidence before public claims, capability-status
discipline, static and accessible delivery, privacy, security, publication
review, draft exclusion, public/private separation, testing and release
approval. This amendment does not supersede the Website Constitution.

## 3. Website v2 semantic architecture

The accepted Website v2 primary architecture is:

- Home — `/`
- Enterprise Reality — `/enterprise-reality/`
- Explorer — `/explorer/`
- Platform — `/platform/`
- Case Studies — `/case-studies/`
- Knowledge — `/knowledge/`
- About — `/about/`

The accepted nested semantic routes are:

- `/explorer/architecture/`
- `/platform/architecture/`
- `/case-studies/damp-and-mould/`
- `/knowledge/canon/`
- `/knowledge/architecture/`
- `/knowledge/publications/`
- `/knowledge/releases/`
- `/knowledge/cde/`
- `/knowledge/research/`

This is a semantic architecture, not an instruction to create empty routes.
Each route shall be implemented only when it has approved, proportionate public
content and the applicable acceptance controls.

The governing public-experience principle is: **Understanding comes before
reference. Reference substantiates understanding.**

The narrative foundation is: Home recognises and orients; Enterprise Reality
explains and defines; Damp & Mould demonstrates and tests that explanation
against a concrete situation.

## 4. Architecture and case-study boundaries

`/platform/architecture/` owns the public explanation of executable Enterprise
Reality platform architecture. `/explorer/architecture/` owns the public
explanation of Explorer product and experience architecture. Neither route
shall collapse the two responsibilities or expose private implementation
material.

Damp & Mould is the canonical Website v2 case. An authorised demonstration may
support a demonstrated-investigation claim only after public classification,
editorial review and publication approval. It shall not be represented as a
customer deployment, commercial availability, housing application, or general
product certification. Existing Website v1 industry scenarios shall not be
promoted into Case Studies without separate product authority.

## 5. Public capability-state projection

Website v2 shall maintain one authoritative public capability-state projection
and consume it wherever the same capability is shown. The projection shall
distinguish:

1. **Capability maturity** — the applicable constitutional status: Released,
   In Development, Planned, Research or Concept.
2. **Programme or release standing** — the status of an approved programme or
   release record; it is not a substitute for capability maturity.
3. **Public or commercial availability** — a separate claim, made only where
   explicitly approved and evidenced.

Explorer shall be represented as an authorised product with
implemented/demonstrated capability, rather than merely Planned. This does not
authorise a commercial-availability claim. No capability shall be represented
as Released merely because it appears in architecture, a demonstration,
implementation evidence, or an engineering certification record.

The projection shall remain the smallest mechanism compatible with the static
Astro website. It shall not introduce a content-management system, a parallel
programme authority, or a dependency on the private programme repository.

## 6. Knowledge boundary

Knowledge shall expose only programme artefacts that have been approved for
public release and are suitable for that purpose. Programme-repository presence,
implementation, demonstration, review, or certification alone does not make an
artefact public. No automatic repository mirroring is authorised.

## 7. Implementation effect

This amendment authorises subsequent Website v2 implementation planning and
implementation within its stated architecture. It does not itself create
routes, components, public copy, availability claims, release certification, or
publication approval for any individual artefact.
